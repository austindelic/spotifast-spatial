Spotifast Spatial 0.14.1-spatial.1 brings the experimental macOS spatial audio fork up to [Spotifast 0.14.1 by @crmne](https://github.com/crmne/spotifast/releases/tag/v0.14.1).

## Updated

- Includes the 159 upstream commits since this fork's original base, through `3ce8b3f` (0.14.1).
- Keeps native AVFoundation stereo spatialization and supported head tracking for compatible AirPods on Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 12.3 or newer.
- Supports upstream's persistent output-device IDs while retaining compatibility with saved device names. Selecting a device pins that output; the system-default setting continues to follow macOS.
- Preserves upstream's time-based playback queue, stream recovery and standard-output underrun diagnostics.

Preview updates continue to come from `austindelic/spotifast-spatial`, using Apple Silicon DMGs and this fork's unsigned checksums. The upstream publisher's signing key cannot authenticate this fork's releases.

This remains an unofficial preview requiring Spotify Premium for local playback. It spatializes stereo audio and does not add Dolby Atmos tracks. The existing 0.12.0-spatial.1 download remains available until a newer preview is published.
